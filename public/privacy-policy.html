Fatima Goat Farm — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 August 2026

Fatima Goat Farm is farm-management software for goat farms. It records a farm's herd, daily work, animal health, feed and medicine stock, staff, sales, money, and the monthly bills for clients whose goats the farm rears. It is a business tool, used by farm owners and their staff.

The app is published by FiveM Infotech. For anything in this policy, contact 5fivempvt@gmail.com.

1. Who the data belongs to

Three kinds of people appear in the app, and they are treated differently:

  • The farm's own team — owner, manager, workers and vet. They sign in, so we hold their account details in order to run the service.
  • Clients — people whose goats the farm rears for a monthly fee. They also sign in, and can see only their own goats and their own bills.
  • People the farm records — staff who do not have a login, and buyers of goats. We store this on the farm's behalf. The farm decides what to enter, and the farm is responsible for having the right to enter it. We do not use it for anything of our own.

2. What is collected

Data Why
Name, email address, mobile number, password (stored hashed) To create and secure an account
Role and permissions To show each person only what their job needs — a worker cannot see the farm's finances
Client details — address, and an identity document type and number (for example Aadhaar) where the farm chooses to record one To identify who owns which goat, since the farm is holding another person's livestock. Optional — a client can be added without it

Fatima Goat Farm is not a health app for people. The health records in it are veterinary records about goats. No human medical data is collected.

3. Permissions the app asks for

  • Camera — to scan the QR tag on a goat's ear tag so its record opens instantly, and to photograph goats and completed work. The camera is never used in the background, and the app does not record audio or video.
  • Photos — only when you choose to attach an existing photograph to a goat, a task or a document. The app reads the single image you pick, and nothing else in your gallery.
  • Internet — the app is a cloud service; data is stored on our servers rather than only on the device, so a worker in the field and an owner at home see the same information.

The app does not request location, microphone, contacts, calendar or SMS access.

4. Payments

A client can pay their monthly bill inside the app. Payments are handled by Razorpay Software Private Limited. Card, UPI and banking details are entered on Razorpay's own payment screen and go directly to Razorpay — they never pass through, and are never stored on, our servers. We receive only the outcome: whether a payment succeeded, its amount, and a reference number, so the invoice can be marked paid.

Payments in this app are for the real-world service of rearing and caring for a physical animal. No digital content is sold.

5. WhatsApp reminders

When a farm sends a client a payment reminder, the app opens WhatsApp on the sender's own phone with the message already written, and the sender taps Send themselves. The message is sent from their WhatsApp account, exactly as if they had typed it. We do not use the WhatsApp Business API and we do not send anything to Meta from our servers.

6. Who else the data is shared with

Fatima Goat Farm does not sell data, does not share it for advertising, and contains no advertising or third-party analytics SDKs. Data is handled by the following service providers, solely to run the service:

Email provider (SMTP) Transactional email only — password reset and account invitations

We may also disclose data where the law requires it, or to establish or defend a legal claim.

7. Security

  • All traffic between the app and our servers uses HTTPS.
  • Passwords are stored hashed with bcrypt; we cannot read them.
  • Access is role-based — the owner decides what each person can see and do, and a client can only ever see their own goats and bills.
  • Sign-in uses short-lived access tokens with refresh tokens, so a stolen token expires quickly.
  • Every farm's data is separated from every other farm's.

No system is perfectly secure. If we become aware of a breach affecting your data, we will notify you.

8. How long data is kept

Farm records are kept for as long as the account is active, because sales, invoices, payroll and animal health history have to be retained for tax and livestock record-keeping purposes. Indian tax rules generally require financial records to be kept for six years, and we retain them accordingly unless deletion is requested.

Sign-in sessions are kept until they expire or the user signs out.

9. Deleting your account and data

You can request deletion of your account and its data at any time:

  • By emailing 5fivempvt@gmail.com from the address on the account, or
  • If you are a member of a farm's team rather than its owner, by asking your owner or manager to remove you under Team.

We will confirm the request and delete the account and its records within 30 days, other than anything we are legally required to keep (for example, tax records still within their statutory retention period). Where that applies, the retained data is not used for any other purpose and is deleted once the period ends.

Full details are at /delete-account.html.

10. Children

Fatima Goat Farm is business software for farm operators. It is not directed at children and is not intended for anyone under 18. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

11. Your rights

You can ask us to give you a copy of your data, correct it, or delete it. Write to 5fivempvt@gmail.com and we will respond within 30 days.
