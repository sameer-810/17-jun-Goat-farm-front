Delete Your Account & Data

Fatima Goat Farm — operated by FiveM Infotech

This page explains how to have your Fatima Goat Farm account and its data deleted, what gets removed, and what we are obliged to keep.

How to request deletion

  1. Email 5fivempvt@gmail.com from the email address on the account, with the subject "Delete my Fatima Goat Farm account".
  2. Include your farm's name so we can identify the right records.
  3. We reply to confirm — deletion is irreversible, so we check before acting.
  4. The account and its data are deleted within 30 days.

Request account deletion

If you are a worker, vet, manager or client rather than the farm's owner, we will confirm with the owner before deleting anything shared — one person cannot erase a farm's records. To simply remove your own access, ask the owner or manager to remove you under Team in the app; that takes effect immediately and needs no email to us.

How long it takes

We acknowledge within 3 working days and complete deletion within 30 days of confirming your request. Backups are rotated on a 30-day cycle, so data may persist in an encrypted backup for up to 30 days after deletion before being overwritten.

Payment records

If you have paid a bill through the app, Razorpay holds its own record of that transaction as the payment processor. That record is governed by Razorpay's privacy policy and their statutory retention obligations, and is outside our control. We delete our copy of the invoice and payment reference as described above, subject to the tax-retention exception.

Removing one person instead of the whole farm

If you only want to remove one person's access rather than the entire farm's records, the owner or manager can do that in the app under Team without contacting us. That person's completed tasks and audit history stay on the farm's records, which is what an audit trail is for.
